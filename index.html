FAQ

Questions, answered

Is 1Save free?

Yes. You can save links, images and notes, make folders, set reminders and lock a secret folder for free. Premium adds unlimited links and folders, hidden secret folders and cloud backup and sync.

Which apps can I save from?

Any app with a Share button: TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X, Chrome and more. You can also copy a link and open 1Save, or paste it in yourself.

Where is my data stored?

1Save is local-first: your links live on your phone. Premium users can back up and sync to the cloud with their Google account. Secret folders always stay on your device.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. 1Save is available on Android through Google Play.

How do I delete my account?

Use the account deletion form and we’ll remove your account and its data. Questions? Email support@1save.app.
